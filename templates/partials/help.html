Help

Getting around

  • Move the pointer to the left edge for the Loops drawer, to the right edge for Structures.
  • Right-click BLOOM for the session menu and a tab's name for its menu.
  • Space plays and stops.

Editing a track

  • Click a track at the bottom to open its grid. Click again to mute it.
  • Click and drag on the grid to add a note; right-click and drag to select notes.
  • Scroll to change the duration of selected notes. Backspace deletes them.
  • Play the keyboard with A W S E D F T G Y H U J; Z/X change octave. A MIDI keyboard works too.
  • ←/→ switch between automation, studio and grid views; Esc goes back.

Your work

Everything is saved automatically in this browser. Download a backup, or move a session to another computer, from the BLOOM menu.
