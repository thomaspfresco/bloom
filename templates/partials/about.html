BLOOM

BLOOM (2023–2024) — made with (too much) love by Thomas Fresco.

A dissertation project in the context of the Master in Design and Multimedia, Department of Informatics Engineering of the Faculty of Sciences and Technology of the University of Coimbra.

Loops are sketched on a grid, extended with bass, harmony and drum suggestions from small recurrent neural networks that run entirely in your browser, and arranged into structures. The “Oblique Strategies” on the home screen are by Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt.
